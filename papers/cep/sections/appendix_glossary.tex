\section{Glossary of sugar-process terms (English / Slovak)}\label{app:glossary}

Terms as used in this paper, with the Slovak names used at the Sere\v{d} plant (DCS screens, lab sheets, operator
notes) so that plant staff and authors read the same thing. Czech terms are marked (cz). Sources: [GH]
\citet{GeblerHotovy2020,GeblerHotovy2021}; [P] Slovensk\'e cukrovary plant Q\&A and DCS screens
\citep{SlovenskeCukrovary2026}; [Be] \citet{Bennar2010}. The terms are listed in \tabref{tab:glossary}.
