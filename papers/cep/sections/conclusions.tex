\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We examined what the camera installed at the sight glass of the Sere\v{d} seed pan can measure before any detector
is trusted. The picture is an analogue PAL signal digitised by the recorder; the 1080p clips are screen recordings
of it and add no resolution. A source-classed model of this chain gives a pixel scale of $1.464 \times 1.913$~\um{}
per clip pixel and $3.92 \times 3.59$~\um{} per PAL sample at the focused plane, with an input-uncertainty budget of
about $\pm 10$\,\% at 95\,\% if the sensor is 1/3\,in, the recorder samples at 13.5~MHz and the tube is
${\approx}\,60$~mm (RQ1). The budget is not a validation: no object of known size has been imaged through the glass,
and a first matched-time comparison with the microscope disagrees (\secref{sec:validation}). We therefore report no
absolute crystal size from the camera; camera growth is treated in pixels, its scale to be identified by the
microscope anchor (\secref{sec:growth}). At F/1.6 the in-focus slice is $\pm 9.4$~\um, and
per-pixel sharpness statistics are sensitive to the recorder overlays and the pillarbox, which must be cropped
(RQ2). In so thin a slice a single focal-plane scale suffices, and for objects thinner than the slice a single-component
size prior cannot tell front from back: within the admitted depth range the two hypotheses differ by at most
0.17\,\%, and the near/far labels are tie-breaks (RQ3). The edge-detector distribution depends on its area window,
the watershed tiles the frame and follows its seed clearance, and over a full PAL strike both medians stay flat from
before seeding to discharge, so neither measures crystal size (RQ4).

The seed-pan microscope series of 2026-10-30, with median chords of operator-picked crystals from 29~\um{} to 483~\um{}
122~min after seeding, defines the range a camera size measurement must cover and the independent reference that a growth-curve
calibration needs. The next steps are a segmentation validated against hand-labelled crystals
(\secref{sec:validation}) and site measurements of the camera model, the tube and the scale at the inner glass face
(\secref{sec:recs}); with both, a camera size signal can enter the growth-state soft sensor of \secref{sec:softsensor}. The
code, configuration and number-generating scripts are released open source (Appendix~\ref{app:repro}).
