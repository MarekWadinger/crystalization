## 2. Observability and practical identifiability (SIMULATED)

State `x = [ln L, ln G, θ]`, process `L⁺ = L + G·Δt`, camera `y = ln L + θ + v`, anchor `z = ln L + w`. The map `(L, G, θ) → (cL, cG, θ − ln c)` leaves every camera output unchanged (unit test, exact to 1e-12), so its tangent `[1, 1, −1]` is unobservable.

Design note: the brief specified G as a random walk. In `[ln L, G, θ]` coordinates the unobservable direction is `[1, G, −1]` and moves with the estimate of G, so the EKF linearised at noisy estimates gains spurious θ information (the EKF inconsistency known from SLAM). With `ln G` the direction is constant and the EKF keeps σ_θ at its prior (unit test, diffuse priors, tolerance 0.1 %).

## 3. Leave-one-strike-out, SIMULATED camera signal

Signal: `y_k = ln L_micro(t_k) + θ_true + v_k` once per minute while L_micro ≥ 40 µm; L_micro = test strike's microscope medians, ln-linear between samples; θ_true ~ N(0, 0.16²) per seed; v_k ~ N(0, σ_v²); 200 seeds per row. Anchor (where used) = the first test sample at t ≥ 30 min, z = ln median + w, w ~ N(0, 0.1²); the anchor-only references get the same w per seed. EKF prior from the training strike (ln L(0) = its first median, SD 1.0; ln G(0) = ln of its mean growth rate, SD 1.0; θ ~ N(0, 0.16²)); Q_G tuned on the training strike (table at the end).

References without camera: (i) the nominal curve = training strike's interpolated medians; (ii) anchor only, scale: nominal shifted in ln L to pass through the anchor; (iii) anchor only, age: nominal shifted in time to pass through the anchor (growth age at the anchor). Before the anchor (ii) and (iii) equal (i). A DCS process-signal baseline cannot run: no DCS data exist for 2026-10-30.

Metrics at the held-out microscope times of the test strike (b1: 49, 60, 79, 96, 122, 160, 170 min; b2: 52, 69, 96, 146, 162 min; anchor excluded): log-size RMSE; growth-rate RMSE against a ±10-min central difference of the microscope curve; G/L RMSE; growth-age RMSE. Mean [5 %, 95 %] over seeds.

Flags (rule, both kinds): on the 1-min grid, flag slower when d + 1.96·σ_d < −ln 1.2 (faster: d − 1.96·σ_d > ln 1.2) on 10 consecutive points; the flag time is the last of them (the online decision time). Size flag: d = ln L̂ − ln L_nom(t), σ_d = posterior SD of ln L. G/L flag (scale-free): d = ln(Ĝ/L̂) − ln(G_nom/L_nom)(t), σ_d = posterior SD of ln G − ln L; θ cancels. References carry only the anchor's SD. Cells: % of seeds flagged / median flag time (min since seeding).

Circularity: the SIMULATED signal is built from the same microscope medians it is scored against. These rows test the estimator's mechanics against a known truth; they do not test the camera.

Growth age τ̂ = the time at which the nominal curve reaches L̂, defined on the nominal's monotone part (first sample up to its maximum; the b1 decline 486 → 452 µm is cut). It is ill-conditioned on the b1 plateau: on the b1 curve τ(483 µm) = 122.5 min, τ(484 µm) = 137.7 min, τ(485.5 µm) = 160.4 min, so 1 % in size moves τ by tens of minutes. It is undefined above the nominal's maximum (b1 485.5 µm, b2 399.0 µm): with b2 as nominal, the b1 samples at 122–170 min have no growth age. τ RMSE is over the times where both τ̂ and τ are defined; the count is given.

## 6. Limitations

- n = 2 strikes, one sampled run (2026-10-30). Leave-one-strike-out tunes on
  one strike and tests on the other; nothing here estimates between-strike
  or between-day variability. With two strikes, "slower than nominal" for
  one is "faster" for the other by construction.
- Microscope truth is one AI transcription of burned-in overlay values
  (no second reading), of operator-chosen crystals (not a size
  distribution); photo clock ±47 s.
- SIMULATED camera: built from the microscope curve itself, with the
  brief's scratch noise levels (σ_v 0.10 / 0.18, not from a repo script).
  Section 3 has no bias β(L), no outliers and a constant θ; section 4
  probes β(L) and a drifting θ with chosen magnitudes. It tests the
  estimator, not the camera.
- REAL camera: 12 (frame, reader) medians from 6 frames at 2 sample times
  of one strike, traced by AI annotators (not a detector); the Cellpose
  detector that would give a 1/min signal is not trained yet.
- No DCS data for 2026-10-30, so the process-signal baseline cannot run.
- Growth rates (truth and nominal) are ±10-min central differences of
  interpolated medians; G/L flags inherit their noise.
- Decision-rule constants (20 %, 1.96 σ, 10 points), the 40 µm floor, the
  priors, the anchor time, L_ref and Q_θ are analysis choices, not tuned.
