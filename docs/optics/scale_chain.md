## Empirical checks (no datasheet input)

**(a) OSD lever arm → display stretch.** The DVR draws the same date and `CH01` glyphs into both recordings. Each PAL glyph box is resampled and located in the clip at sub-pixel precision; the box-to-box displacement over the PAL lever arm (115 samples, 503 lines) is the stretch, iterated to a fixed point. 3 PAL frames × 3 clip frames (t = 5, 20, 40 s) per clip.

| Clip | stretch x | stretch y | x offset (px) | min NCC |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 10-18-50 | 2.6781 ± 0.0007 | 1.8755 ± 0.0001 | -0.9 | 0.78 |
| 10-36-32 | 2.6783 ± 0.0007 | 1.8755 ± 0.0002 | -0.9 | 0.84 |
| 10-37-26 | 2.6756 ± 0.0004 | 1.8754 ± 0.0001 | +9.3 | 0.79 |

All clips: x 2.6773 (range 2.6752–2.6795), y 1.8755 (range 1.8752–1.8756); config `display_1080p.stretch_x/_y` = 2.677, 1.875. A plain 720 → 1920 scaler would be 2.6667; the y stretch is 1080/576 = 1.8750. The x offset is where PAL column 0 lands; it differs between clips (the whole picture shifts by a few samples), which moves features but not their scale.

**Picture edges** (half-level, sub-pixel; camera frames only). PAL recording: left 15.5, right 700.3 (first minute, 25 frames). The left edge is a hard step (a DVR mask), the right edge a 4-sample analog ramp.

| Clip | camera frames | left edge | right edge (min–max) | left / stretch_x |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 10-18-50 | 25/25 | 42.4 | 1869.5–1869.9 | 15.85 |
| 10-36-32 | 25/25 | 42.4 | 1869.5–1877.9 | 15.85 |
| 10-37-26 | 23/25 | 42.4 | 1877.6–1878.0 | 15.85 |

The clips' left edge is PAL column 16 × stretch_x, so the pillarbox starts at the DVR mask. The right edge jumps by ~8 px (~3 samples), between clips and within 10-36-32 (near t = 45 s), because the analog picture shifts against the DVR's sampling; the width between the two edges therefore is not a scale measurement, and the auto-crop of `optics.video_io` (threshold 12, all frames) is not either: on 10-37-26 it includes the non-camera tail of the clip.

**(b) Spectral isotropy → pixel aspect.** µm_x/µm_y from the power-spectrum ellipse of the slurry texture (25 frames per recording; bands in cycles per PAL line, so one object band on both recordings). Chain prediction: PAL 1.093, 1080p 0.766. The last column converts the clips' mean into a PAL value through the measured stretch (× stretch_x / stretch_y), without the datasheet.

| Band (cyc/line) | PAL direct | 1080p 10-18-50 | 1080p 10-36-32 | 1080p 10-37-26 | PAL via 1080p |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0.005–0.03 | 1.002 | 0.625 | 0.662 | 0.664 | 0.928 |
| 0.01–0.05 | 1.065 | 0.705 | 0.728 | 0.758 | 1.042 |
| 0.02–0.08 | 1.132 | 0.772 | 0.768 | 0.815 | 1.121 |

The spectral ratio rises with the band on every recording (illumination gradients at the lowest frequencies, anisotropic analog bandwidth, interlace and flow blur at the higher ones). It brackets the chain prediction but cannot pin the pitch better than about ±10 %: a consistency check, not a measurement. On synthetic power-law textures with a known squeeze (tests/test_scale_chain.py) the estimator is within 5 % in the middle band, and biased by up to +10 % in the lowest band when the spectrum is shallow.
